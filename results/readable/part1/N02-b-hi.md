   - Dark spots aur dullness ko aur badhane se **SPF 50+** lagana zaroori hai. Serum ke saath **broad-spectrum sunscreen** use karo.

4. **Consistency is Key:**  
   - Serum ko **daily 2-3 mahine** tak use karo. Results 4-6 week mein dikhna shuru ho jate hain.

5. **Skin Type ke Hisab se Choose Karo:**  
   - **Oily Skin:** Salicylic Acid ya Niacinamide  
   - **Dry Skin:** Vitamin C + Hyaluronic Acid + Oils  
   - **Sensitive Skin:** Licorice, Turmeric, jojoba oil
