- **Subah:** serum → moisturizer → **SPF 50 sunscreen**. Bahar ho toh sunscreen 2–3 ghante mein reapply karo.
- **Raat:** gentle cleanser → moisturizer.

Serum pehle hafte alternate days lagao aur patch test kar lo. Glow aane mein aam taur par **6–8 weeks** lag sakte hain; sunscreen ke bina tan wapas hota rahega. Skin oily, dry ya sensitive hai? Us hisaab se dono mein se better pick bata sakta hoon.
