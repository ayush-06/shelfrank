### **Why Your Skin Feels Stretched and Flaky in Winter**

Winter weather strips your skin of natural moisture due to:
- **Low humidity** (dry air)
- **Harsh winds** that remove surface oils
- **Indoor heating** that exacerbates dryness
- **Cold temperatures** that slow down skin cell turnover

When skin loses moisture, it can feel tight, stretched, and develop flaky patches — a condition known as **xerosis** (dry skin). The goal is to **restore the skin barrier**, **lock in moisture**, and **soothe irritation**.
