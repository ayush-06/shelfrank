**Practical suggestions**
- **Mild dandruff:** Head & Shoulders (Clinic Plus and Dove also make anti-dandruff variants).
- **Persistent dandruff:** Try Nizral or Ketomac (ketoconazole) 2-3 times a week for 3-4 weeks, then once a week for maintenance.
- **Dry or sensitive scalp:** Choose a gentler formula, and avoid washing too often with harsh products.

**Tips for better results**
- Leave the shampoo on your scalp for **3-5 minutes** before rinsing, since it needs contact time to work.
- **Rotate** between two different active ingredients if one stops working.
- Wash regularly. Oil left on the scalp, especially in humid weather, can worsen dandruff.
- Avoid scratching, and clean combs and pillow covers regularly.

**See a dermatologist if**
- There's no improvement after 4 weeks
- Your scalp is very red, itchy, painful, or has thick patches or sores
