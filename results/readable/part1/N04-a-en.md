- **Salicylic Acid / BHA** – penetrates pores to clear oil & dead skin
- **Niacinamide (Vitamin B3)** – reduces oil & inflammation
- **Zinc PCA** – regulates sebum production
- **Tea Tree Oil** – natural antibacterial
- **Glycolic / Lactic Acid** – gentle exfoliation

### **2. Avoid These Ingredients (if sensitive):**
- **Heavy oils (coconut oil, olive oil)** – can clog pores
- **Alcohol-heavy** – can strip skin and cause rebound oiliness
- **Fragrance (if sensitive)** – can irritate

### **3. Texture Matters**
- **Gel or Foaming** – best for oily skin
- **Avoid heavy creams** – can trap oil

### **4. Frequency of Use**
- **Twice a day (morning & night)** is ideal
- **Don’t over-cleanse** – can damage skin barrier
